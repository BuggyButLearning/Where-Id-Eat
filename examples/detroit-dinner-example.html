Dinner picks

1. Selden Standard

Seasonal New American • 3921 2nd Ave • ~6–9 min ride
Fit 9.6Solo: strong

Why: the safest top-tier Detroit meal without feeling formal. The open hearth and constantly changing menu create a strong reason to go beyond convenience.

Order: build around a vegetable plate, pasta, and one meat or fish dish.

WebsiteYelpEater

2. Takoi

Modern Thai • 2520 Michigan Ave • ~7–10 min ride
Fit 9.3Solo: strong

Why: high-contrast Thai cooking that feels much more singular than a generic upscale dinner.

Order: khao soi, naem khao tod, or the current tasting path.

WebsiteYelpLocal review

3. Baobab Fare

Burundian / East African • 6568 Woodward Ave • ~9–13 min ride
Fit 9.2Value standout

Why: one of the hardest meals on the list to reproduce back home, with major local and national recognition.

Order: Nyumbani or Mbuzi, plus sambusas if hungry.

WebsiteYelpEater

4. Chartreuse Kitchen & Cocktails

Seasonal small plates • 15 E Kirby St • ~8–11 min ride
Fit 9.1

Why: ingredient-focused cooking with a long Detroit track record and practical bar seating.

Order: the twice-cooked egg plus whatever vegetable and main look strongest that night.

WebsiteYelp

5. Leila

Modern Lebanese • 1245 Griswold St • ~3 min walk
Fit 8.9Very close

Why: strong mezze, current local recognition, and a menu that scales well for a solo diner.

Order: baba ghanouj, batata harra, and either arayes or shish tawook.

WebsiteYelpEater

How this guide works

Directions from the hotel

Food quality and specificity come first. Current local support, value, travel time from the anchor, and solo practicality follow. Same-day availability is part of ranking, not a correction appendix. Yelp is treated as a useful community signal, not the only authority.
